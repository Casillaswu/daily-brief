01 · LEAD Sports −Negative US · Handle plateau

H2 projects NFL handle at $31.4bn this season, down 0.8%, the first in-season decline in nine years.

Covers · H2 Gambling Capital · Legal Sports Report · 09/08–09/09

On 9 September H2 Gambling Capital projected NFL sportsbook handle of $31.4bn for the season running August to February, down 0.8% on last year. Including college football the whole market comes to $42.1bn, with gross win of $3.5bn, down 2%. This is the first in-season decline since the United States opened up sports betting in 2018. Underlying NFL demand is still growing around 4%; the decline comes from two things, unusually favourable operator luck last year and prediction markets taking handle away.

H2 estimates prediction markets are taking 4% to 6% of sportsbook handle in states that already have legal online betting. On a handle-equivalent basis, event contracts already account for roughly 35% of the combined sportsbook and prediction-market pool, and 70% to 80% of that volume sits in states with no regulated online betting. Three figures line up against that: the American Gaming Association puts legal NFL handle at $29.5bn, essentially flat on last season's $29.4bn; Kalshi, the prediction-market venue licensed by the Commodity Futures Trading Commission, set a single-day record of $2.293bn in notional volume on 5 September, of which 58.6% was parlays, so its revenue mix now leans on the same high-margin product sportsbooks rely on; and New York online sports betting handle was $1.73bn in August, down 15.2% year on year and the weakest month since July 2025.


Bottom line: Put together, the conclusion is that the US handle curve has flattened rather than continuing to grow. H2's 4% to 6% only counts direct diversion inside the same state. The real volume sits in states with no regulated online betting, where the money was never going to reach a licensed book, so the total pool is additive while the licensed share is being squeezed. −EV for sportsbooks already licensed in the US, +EV for prediction-market venues and content suppliers. Two things to act on: drop market growth as an assumption in acquisition budgets and set CPA ceilings from same-state share instead; and note H2's point that every 0.05 of hold is worth about $150m of NFL gross win, so there is very little room to make the year on luck, which means promotional intensity has to be read alongside hold rather than handle alone.

SIGNAL 02 · US · TWO LOSSES IN A WEEK

Kalshi lost in Iowa and the Tenth Circuit, and Connecticut subpoenaed 29 suppliers.

Two adverse rulings landed on 8 September. Iowa federal judge Stephen Locher denied Kalshi's preliminary injunction, holding that exclusive jurisdiction under the Commodity Exchange Act reaches only swaps and futures, and quoted Kalshi's own advertising line about being the first app for legal sports betting in all 50 states, calling the situation a problem of the company's own making. The same day the Tenth Circuit denied Kalshi's emergency injunction in Utah, leaving it exposed to third-degree felony risk there. On 10 September Connecticut's Department of Consumer Protection issued cease-and-desist orders to nine platforms including Polymarket, Coinbase, Crypto.com, Robinhood and Underdog Predict, and served subpoenas on 29 parties including PayPal, Plaid, Sportradar, Genius Sports, the Apple App Store, Google Play, Stripe and 15 media outlets such as ESPN. Kalshi's sports contracts are now barred in six states.


Bottom line: The notable part is not another loss but Connecticut extending its reach to suppliers and media. Enforcement used to stop at the platform, which could keep trading while it litigated. Now payments, data, app stores and ad sellers have all been served, which pushes each of those companies to decide for itself whether the account is worth keeping. That works without winning the underlying case. −EV for prediction-market venues, +EV for sportsbooks holding state licences. Practical step for cross-border deals: any partner pitching on the basis that it is an exchange rather than a casino should prompt a check of where you sit in the chain, because payments, data, creative services and traffic referral can all be treated as supply. Jurisdiction and termination clauses need updating now. Read original →

SIGNAL 03 · BRAZIL · EXECUTIVE DECREE

Brazil is preparing a decree that may ban the online casino category outright.

Reporting on 8 September said the federal government has chosen a provisional measure, which takes effect on signature and goes to Congress afterwards, rather than an ordinary bill. The Casa Civil is leading it, the draft is due to reach Congress next week, and it includes outright bans on specific categories, with online casino among those under assessment. The Finance Ministry is resisting restrictions severe enough to damage companies that are already licensed. Internal polling for the governing party shows three in four Brazilians oppose betting platforms, with higher figures among younger voters and women. On 10 September Lula, Brazil's current president, told SBT News that he has catalogued the views from the 1 September Planalto meeting and will decide on that basis, while publicly doubting tax as a tool: tax only bites when it hurts profit, and when tax amounts to legalising a wrong, companies pay it without difficulty. He also noted that many operators run remotely with no Brazilian head office. Brazil votes for president, governors and Congress on 4 October.


Bottom line: The president dismissing tax, the executive choosing an instrument that takes effect immediately, and the Casa Civil rather than the Finance Ministry running it all point the same way: what comes next is more likely to be product and advertising bans than a rate change. A tax can be modelled. A product category disappearing next week cannot. −EV for online casino products in Brazil, smaller impact on pure sports betting and operators with a spread of categories. Three things to do now: split Brazilian revenue by product category and work out the online casino share; pull advertising and club sponsorship contracts to check for regulatory-change termination clauses; and prepare a version of the creative with brand exposure removed. SIGNAL 04 · PHILIPPINES · FIRST RATING

Moody's rates DigiPlus B1 and expects 2026 EBITDA down 20.3% this year.

On 10 September Moody's Ratings assigned DigiPlus Interactive a first-time B1 corporate family rating with a stable outlook. It expects EBITDA to fall from PHP14.2bn in 2025 to about PHP11.4bn in 2026, down 20.3%, recovering to PHP14bn to PHP15bn across 2027 and 2028. Moody's names the cause directly: the central bank's August 2025 instruction requiring e-wallets and payment firms to sever in-app links to online gambling platforms, which lowered online gross gaming revenue across the industry, compounded by high fuel prices and inflation weighing on disposable income. DigiPlus second-quarter EBITDA was already down 36.9% year on year, on a market share of about 38.5% and roughly six million monthly active users. The important caveat is that the rating does not assume a national online gambling ban; if one passes, a multi-notch downgrade would follow. The stock closed at PHP8.76 on 11 September.


Bottom line: This is the first time an international rating agency has put the Philippine online decline into a formal document, and the cause it names is the payment rails rather than demand. Two things follow. The 20.3% figure is a pricing anchor for negotiations: the market leader's own earnings forecast is shrinking, so any revenue share or guarantee based on last year's numbers should come down. And the rating explicitly excludes a ban, which is an admission that the tail risk cannot be priced, while the Senate saw another full-ban push this week, so Philippine contracts need an exit triggered by a ban. +EV for groups with land-based assets and payment relationships, −EV for online-only brands dependent on e-wallet deposits. SIGNAL 05 · CAMBODIA · NATIONWIDE HALT

Cambodia suspends online gambling at all land-based casinos from October.

Deputy Prime Minister and Interior Minister Sar Sokha announced on 5 September, at a public event in Takeo province, that Cambodia will suspend online gambling operations at every land-based casino in the country from October, on the instruction of Prime Minister Hun Manet. The stated reason is that live-streamed casino gambling makes money flows hard to monitor and hampers the campaign against technology-enabled crime. During the suspension ministries are to build a legal framework, supervisory measures, digital infrastructure and specialist staffing before a resumption is considered; operators that do not comply can lose their licence and face prosecution. The basis is the Law on the Management of Commercial Gambling, and Cambodia has issued no new online-only licences since 2019. The same week the anti-scam committee confirmed 18 casino licences revoked and nine more suspended, having inspected 195 licensed casinos and flagged 72 for suspected online fraud since July 2025.


Bottom line: This is the largest supply-side change in Asia this week: a whole market's online traffic disappears in October, by administrative order and with no transition period. The parties most directly affected are platform and content suppliers using Cambodia as an operating base or a traffic source. +EV for licensed Philippine operators, −EV for platforms and agencies domiciled in Cambodia: the demand that goes dark will move to other jurisdictions, with the Philippines and some offshore sites the nearest substitutes, and it will also push that pool of operations and support staff onto the labour market, making September to December the cheapest hiring window. The check to run now is whether any supplier on your list is domiciled in Cambodia, and to re-verify licence status after 1 October. Read original →

Regulation−NegativeUK · Machine duty doubling

UK may double all three machine games duty bands on 28 October.

SBC News · The Times · Racing Post · 09/07

The Times reported first on 7 September and the trade press followed: the Treasury is modelling a doubling of all three machine games duty bands, the lower rate from 5% to 10%, the standard rate from 20% to 40% and the higher rate from 25% to 50%, with the budget set for 28 October and nothing decided. Analysts estimate a doubling would close close to 3,000 betting shops and cut the horse racing levy by about £70m. This sits on top of two rises already confirmed: remote gaming duty went from 21% to 40% in April, and general betting duty on remote sports betting goes from 15% to 25% in April 2027. The same week Flutter said it is assessing the closure of around 100 Paddy Power shops.


Bottom line: The UK has moved from a one-off rise to a rise every year for three years, and the target has walked from online towards retail. −EV for UK retail betting and machine revenue, limited impact on online-only brands with no UK exposure. The usable point is the tempo: roughly six months from announcement to commencement, which means adjusting when the story breaks rather than when the statute lands. For payback periods in any mature market, the UK has now demonstrated that the rules get rewritten inside two years, so price the payback on two years.

Regulation○NeutralGlobal · AML risk indicators

FATF issues its first gambling money-laundering review since 2009.

SBC News · iGaming Business · FATF · 09/09–09/10

The Financial Action Task Force (FATF) published a report on 9 September, its first dedicated examination of money-laundering and terrorist-financing risk in gambling since 2009, drawing on questionnaire responses from more than 80 jurisdictions, written submissions from 29 and industry consultation. It concludes that land-based and online casinos and sports betting carry the highest exposure, and that illegal and unlicensed offshore gambling now matches or exceeds the legal market in many countries. The risk-indicator list covers deposits from multiple third-party accounts, heavy reliance on cash or virtual assets, betting on every possible outcome, automated betting patterns, repeated VPN use, multiple accounts under false names, depositing and withdrawing with almost no play, white-label arrangements without oversight, and software, marketing and consultancy contracts with no commercial rationale.


Bottom line: The practical effect is to hand every regulator a ready-made audit script, and the questions already reach white label and marketing consultancy. −EV for white-label and affiliate intermediaries, +EV for operators with their own licence and simple money flows. Three things to prepare: evidence of continuous oversight of white-label partners rather than a signature at the start; marketing and consultancy contracts that can demonstrate commercial rationale, meaning scope, pricing basis and proof of delivery; and readiness for these questions in the next audit cycle, since the EU applies FATF standards.

Regulation−NegativeUK · Affiliate penalised

UK ASA faults affiliate Smart Gravity over a gambling-help search ad.

SBC News · ASA ruling · 09/09–09/10

The Advertising Standards Authority published a ruling on 9 September finding Smart Gravity Ltd in breach of CAP Code rule 1.3. The ad promoted the comparison site top10onlinecasinoreviews.co.uk and appeared against the search term "help with gambling" on 18 June 2026, with copy offering the UK's top ten casino sites, fast signups and mega bonuses. The ASA held that someone searching that term is looking for harm-reduction information and is likely to include vulnerable people, so fast signups and large bonuses are not responsibly targeted. Smart Gravity argued it maintains a negative keyword list to block addiction and safer-gambling terms; the ASA found that a list which fails to block is still a breach.


Bottom line: The direct read-through for paid search is that a negative keyword list is not a defence; if it does not block, you are liable. −EV for affiliates and media agencies. Three things to do now: rebuild the full variant set for terms such as help, addiction and self-exclusion, including misspellings and near matches; keep monthly screenshots of live placements as evidence the list is actually running; and note that phrases like fast signup and large bonus are aggravating factors in a harm-reduction context, so generic comparison-site copy needs splitting by keyword group.
