01 · LEAD Regulation −Negative Brazil · Decree dated

Lula will sign a provisional decree on 23 September banning online casino and slots, while sports betting stays.

Brasil de Fato · Yogonet Brasil · iGaming Brazil · Reuters · 09/18–09/19

Several Brazilian outlets confirmed on 18 September that Lula (Brazil's sitting president) will sign a medida provisória on 23 September — an instrument that takes effect on signature and is ratified by Congress afterwards. The direction differs from the blanket ban reported a week earlier: online casino and online slots are banned (including the products known locally as Tigrinho), sports betting is not, but advertising faces tighter limits. Reuters, citing presidential palace sources, says the final text must reach Lula before the first round of voting on 4 October; Brazil currently has 188 licensed operators. On 18 September in Guarulhos, São Paulo state, Lula used the first person for the first time — "I have decided, I am going to end the bets" — and told the clubs Brazil won five World Cups without bets.

The finance ministry is publicly out of step. Finance Minister Dario Durigan said on 17 September that "no decision has been taken; when there is one we will announce it," framing the issue around household income and cost of living. Two hard constraints have surfaced. Banning online casino may require the government to refund operators a proportion of the R$ 30 million licence fee already paid, and under Brazil's fiscal responsibility law any revenue waiver must come with offsetting measures. The government is therefore weighing a 180-day transition so Congress can review the decree before the new rules bite. The industry estimates online casino accounts for roughly 80% of licensed operators' revenue.


Bottom line: This is the first week the thread has had both a scope and a deadline, rather than campaign rhetoric alone. Narrowing it to casino and slots arguably makes it more likely to land, because sports betting and club sponsorship sit outside the scope — which strips the politically heaviest part of the opposition, the clubs and the broadcasters, of half its motive. Limited effect on pure sports betting and diversified operators, −EV for Brazilian online casino products. Three things can be finished this week: split Brazilian revenue by product category and work out the online casino share; pull the force majeure and regulatory change clauses out of sponsorship and advertising contracts and read them; and if a casino vertical carries Brazilian exposure, flag it separately and watch the gazette around 4 October for a decree number. If the 180-day transition holds, creative need not come down at once, but new long-term deals should push their start dates back.

SIGNAL 02 · US · THREE LOSSES

The Ninth Circuit ruled for two California tribes, Missouri served six prediction markets, and the CLARITY vote failed 49 to 50.

On 17 September the Ninth Circuit reversed the district court and found that Blue Lake Rancheria and Chicken Ranch Rancheria of Me-Wuk Indians are likely to succeed on the claim that Kalshi's sports contracts violate the Indian Gaming Regulatory Act. Two findings are worth copying down: sports event contracts accessed from tribal land constitute Class III gaming on that land, and every contract is an unauthorised bet or wager; and the exclusive-jurisdiction clause of the Commodity Exchange Act, even treating the contracts as swaps, does not exempt them from other federal law. The case returns to the district court on the remaining injunction elements.


Bottom line: The federal route is closed for now. The CLARITY failure means prediction markets get no congressional endorsement this year, and the Ninth Circuit ruling knocks a piece out of the core exclusive-jurisdiction defence: not only states can regulate, tribes can too, and on the authority of a different federal statute. −EV for prediction market platforms, +EV for state-licensed sportsbooks and tribal operators. The assumption to change: deals struck on the premise that a federal licence overrides state law now need costing state by state, and in some cases tribe by tribe. Jurisdiction clauses should list tribal land separately — a parallel permitting layer with its own test. Read original →

SIGNAL 03 · PHILIPPINES · CHANNELISATION HALVED

PAGCOR says channelisation to licensed sites fell from 75% to about 50%, and the central bank closed over 8,000 merchant accounts.

PAGCOR (the Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corporation, which both licenses operators and runs its own land-based casinos) disclosed at this week's IAG EXPO that channelisation to licensed sites now runs at about 50%, against roughly 75% before the central bank required e-wallets and payment providers to delink from gambling platforms in 2025. That is 25 percentage points of traffic moving to the grey market within a year. The market itself is contracting in parallel: Philippine gross gaming revenue fell 20.33% year on year to PHP 88.13 billion in the second quarter of 2026, after a 15.87% fall to PHP 87.60 billion in the first.


Bottom line: Read the two numbers side by side and the conclusion is that compliance cost in the Philippines is converting directly into acquisition cost. Every tightening on the licensed side sends a batch of traffic to the grey market, the licensed player pool shrinks, and the denominator under CPA shrinks with it. +EV for groups with physical assets and direct payment relationships, −EV for pure-online brands dependent on e-wallet deposits. The 2 October comment deadline is the only available lever; acquiring partners will be counted one by one before year end, so confirm now which layer the channel sits in. One more framing fix: at 50% channelisation, a pitch quoting PAGCOR market-wide figures as market size overstates the reachable licensed pool twofold. Read original →

SIGNAL 04 · UK · CUTS BEFORE THE TAX

Entain is cutting 400 UK jobs and left the FTSE 100, citing a possible doubling of machine games duty on 28 October.

Entain (owner of Ladbrokes, Coral and bwin) is cutting up to 400 of the 2,000 roles in its UK customer service function, after 500 cuts in July, and left the FTSE 100 on 18 September. Chief executive Stella David wrote to Greater Manchester mayor Andy Burnham opposing reports that the Chancellor will double all three bands of machine games duty in the 28 October autumn Budget — the lower band from 5% to 10%, the standard band from 20% to 40%, and the higher band from 25% to 50%. She said doubling the standard rate would add roughly £100 million a year to the cost of Entain's retail business, which runs more than 2,300 UK betting shops. Industry-commissioned data estimates a 40% rate would close 1,470 shops and cost 15,900 jobs.


Bottom line: The UK problem is no longer a single tax rate; it is tax and advertising tightening together, and operators are cutting before either is settled. The redundancies come six weeks ahead of the Budget, which means Entain is not waiting for the outcome to restructure its cost base. −EV for UK retail and online operators, +EV for talent and budget competition outside the UK. Two usable windows: senior UK marketing and service staff come onto the market over the next two quarters, so people previously immovable are now reachable; and with UK budgets compressed, competition for international event inventory and affiliate positions eases. For UK exposure, the line to watch in the Lords report is treating influencer promotion as advertising and banning it first — that changes creative formats more directly than any tax rate. SIGNAL 05 · US · MODELLING THE LOSERS

The New York Times found DraftKings scores customers with an elasticity score marking who is worth chasing with free bets.

After interviewing more than 40 former employees and reviewing internal memos, presentations, messages and betting data, the New York Times reported that DraftKings (one of the two largest US sportsbooks) built a machine learning model in 2023 that scores customers on betting frequency, account balance, the ratio of losses to wagers, and an estimate of whether the user might stop gambling altogether. Internally it is called an elasticity score, and a higher score marks a customer as worth pursuing with free bets and bonuses. Executives told the paper that data science and analytics raised promotion-driven sportsbook margin by 13% in 2025, and that AI automated and personalised $400 million of promotional spend.


Bottom line: This one lands closest to marketing work, because it is a methodology story rather than a regulatory one. The industry broadly uses similar segmentation and remarketing logic; the difference is whether expected loss is written as an explicit score. This report names that score, and it will now turn up as ready-made evidence in legislative hearings and litigation. −EV for operations leaning on heavy CRM and personalised promotion, limited effect on brand and advertising-led acquisition. The response is not to switch personalisation off but to audit the retention model's feature list: any feature built on loss amount, loss-to-wager ratio or churn probability should be assumed to require an explanation to regulators in the UK and Brazil. And put the responsible gambling detection model in the same release priority as the retention model — shipping one and shelving the other is precisely what the article alleges. Read original →

Regulation−NegativeUK · Lords report

The House of Lords urges a near-total UK gambling ad ban.

iGaming Business · 09/17

The Lords Liaison Committee published a follow-up to its 2020 inquiry, arguing for a public health framework. Four core recommendations: make the Gambling Commission the statutory authority for advertising instead of the Advertising Standards Authority; ban direct marketing to customers by operators and affiliates, except account and safety notices; ban free bets and sign-up bonuses; and treat content marketing and influencer promotion as advertising, to be banned first. The report cites the Commission's own survey showing 1 to 1.5 million UK adults as high-risk gamblers, and estimates licensed operator advertising spend at £1 billion to £2 billion a year. Committee chair Lord Ponsonby of Shulbrede explicitly rejected the industry argument that advertising limits push consumers to the black market.


Bottom line: The report has no legal force, but it sets the vocabulary for the next two years of legislative debate. The line to watch is treating influencer promotion as advertising and banning it first — if that reaches legislation, UK influencer work does not get dearer, it disappears as a category. −EV for UK affiliates and influencer work, +EV for owned media and brand content. The move now is to keep UK influencer contract terms inside 12 months and avoid signing across the year boundary.

Regulation−NegativeGlobal · Higher bar to advertise

Google tightens advertiser vetting and drops Croatia on 7 October.

SBC News · 09/14–09/15

From 14 September 2026 Google extended the certification bar in its Gambling and Games Policy worldwide, having applied it only to selected markets since March. Two points bite hardest: a Manager Account whose online gambling certification has been revoked repeatedly permanently loses the right to apply again, and existing certifications under it may be revoked with it; and applicants must directly own and control the domain submitted, with free subdomains and unrelated domains refused. The same week Google updated its Croatia country terms to ban all online gambling promotion there from 7 October 2026, revoking existing certifications and accepting no new applications. Google's 2025 ads safety report claims roughly 270.7 million gambling and games ads blocked or removed.


Bottom line: Taken together, these close the "open a new account and start again" route. The old workaround was a fresh Manager Account and a new domain; now the record follows the account, and the domain has to be yours. +EV for clean single-account advertising on owned domains, −EV for multi-account and agency-proxy setups. The audit to run: how many Manager Accounts are in use, which carries the revocation history, and whose name the submitted domains sit under. Croatia carries a second lesson — Google says it is unrelated to the new rules but follows them, so platforms will exit high-risk jurisdictions on their own rather than wait to be forced.

Regulation○NeutralSpain · Who regulates what

Spain's 17 regions oppose a €600 daily cross-operator deposit cap.

SBC News · 09/18

On 7 September Spain's 17 autonomous communities submitted a joint document to the Directorate General for the Regulation of Gambling and the gambling policy council, defending regional jurisdiction over licensing and operating conditions for land-based venues; discussions began on 14 July. Madrid's director general Ramón Cubián leads the group, arguing that the national regulator's bid for a coordinating role over vulnerable-group protection exceeds its powers. In parallel the regulator and the consumer affairs ministry are advancing a national deposit limit system, proposing a €600 daily cap per player across all licensed online operators, a shared risk-behaviour detection algorithm, and separate reporting for players under 21. The industry notes these projects, first proposed in 2023, still have no technical documentation, data specification or interface definition.


Bottom line: The cross-operator deposit cap matters more than it sounds: it turns a player's total exposure across the whole market into a queryable value, so every operator shares one allowance pool. If it ships, lifetime value on high-value segments is hard-capped and acquisition models rebuild around a known per-customer ceiling. −EV for operators built on high-net-worth segments, relatively favourable for volume-and-breadth operators. With no technical documentation and no timeline, nothing needs changing yet; pull the monthly deposit distribution for Spanish players and see what share sits above €600 — that share is the revenue this rule removes.

Market−NegativeMacau · Two years marked down

CLSA cut its 2027 Macau gaming revenue forecast by 4%.

GGRAsia · Inside Asian Gaming · 09/15

CLSA cut its 2027 Macau gross gaming revenue forecast 4% to MOP 259.2 billion (about $32.1 billion) and 2028 by 3% to MOP 270.4 billion, leaving 2026 broadly unchanged at MOP 253.2 billion, implying growth of 2.4% and 4.3%. Analyst Jeffrey Kiang points to weakness in a Chinese industrial profit indicator — the spread between the producer price index and the purchasing price index — which has led year-on-year changes in Macau gross gaming revenue by about six months since July 2005; the spread turned negative in February and widened to minus 3.31 percentage points in August on higher oil prices. Margins are deteriorating in parallel: second-quarter industry property-level operating costs rose 2% year on year while industry gross revenue fell 0.1%, and reported second-quarter EBITDA fell 10.7% to nearly $1.81 billion. Macau international visitors fell 9.8% year on year in August to 171,679, with Philippine arrivals down 18.5% and Japanese down 31.1%.
