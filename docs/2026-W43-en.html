01 · LEAD Regulation −Negative Brazil · Runoff and licences

Brazil's betting licences lapse on runoff day; Flávio says Congress agrees to reopen, Lula says bets won't return.

NEXT.io · BNLData · 10/18–10/24

Provisional Measure 1,394 (MPV 1394) makes every federal and state betting authorisation expire on 25 October, the same day as the presidential runoff. The 85 licensed operators have been dark for almost three weeks since 6 October. Industry body ANJL counts more than 3,500 layoffs and another 4,167 staff on collective leave, and the BRL30 million (about US$5.4M) licence fee per operator will not be refunded. The final Datafolha poll had Flávio Bolsonaro on 47% and Lula on 46%, a 50–50 split of valid votes; Polymarket priced a Flávio win at 79% on 22 October, and BNLData cited 83.7% on the eve of the vote. Votes had not been counted when this issue closed.

The two candidates say opposite things. Lula said in a YouTube live on 22 October that "the bets will not return" and called the sector a "plague". Flávio told Globo's top executives over lunch on 16 October that Congress already has a deal to bring online betting back, with only the inclusion of online slots such as "Tigrinho" still undecided. Globo is BetMGM's Brazilian joint-venture partner and has lost both its own betting income and sports ad revenue; O POVO reports it has cancelled year-end events to save money. Flávio's name also appears in a Federal Police inquiry into money laundering through Best Bet, which he denies. The procedure does not change with the result: Justice Fux will take up the three constitutional challenges (ADIs 8024, 8027 and 8028) only after the vote, with no date set, and the joint committee has still not been installed. The measure locks Congress's agenda from 9 November and first expires on 23 November, extendable by 60 days, so with the recess it could stay in force until March 2027. Flamengo estimates the ban will cost it BRL400–430 million of 2027 revenue.


Bottom line: The election changes the negotiating climate, not the legal position. The authorisations lapsed on 25 October; reopening requires Congress to amend the measure or the Supreme Court to strike it down. Stake's lawyer also says operators will not return "automatically". The main proposal in Congress is still "keep sports betting, ban online casino". For sports-led licensed operators in Brazil, a Flávio win is medium-term +EV and a Lula win is −EV, since the ban could last until March 2027; for casino-led operators, both outcomes are −EV. For the Philippines, Brazil shows that political risk can shut an entire licensed market in two weeks. PAGCOR will launch a licence-check app in December (see §3), so PH operators should proactively submit responsible-gambling data to the regulator. Action: re-assess the Brazil budget and contracts within 48 hours of the result; prepare comeback creative for sports only.

SIGNAL 03 · US · PREDICTION MARKETS

Cruz plans a bill requiring state or tribal consent for prediction-market sports contracts; Polymarket sues Ohio.

SBC Americas, citing Bloomberg Law, reports that Texas Senator Ted Cruz plans to file a bill after the 3 November midterms requiring exchanges registered with the CFTC (Commodity Futures Trading Commission), such as Kalshi, to obtain state or tribal approval before offering event contracts tied to gambling such as sports betting. Today they operate nationwide under the federal framework. Texas has no legal casinos or sports betting, so the bill would push sports contracts out of Texas. It is not yet on the Congress website, and if it does not move in the lame-duck session it must be reintroduced next Congress. In the same week, Polymarket US (QCX LLC) sued Ohio's attorney general Andy Wilson and the seven members of the Casino Control Commission in federal court on 16 October, arguing that the state's licensing regime discriminates against out-of-state firms in breach of the Commerce Clause and is preempted by the Commodity Exchange Act. The complaint cites two CFTC actions of 9 October to argue that the legal landscape has changed since the Sixth Circuit ruled against Kalshi in September. Ohio has sent cease-and-desist letters to 10 companies, and Governor DeWine says the state will sue those that keep operating.


Bottom line: Support for state regulation of prediction markets now includes Republican Senate Commerce Committee chair Cruz, alongside anti-gambling Democrats. If the bill takes shape, "register with the CFTC and operate nationwide" becomes state-by-state approval, the same entry requirements sportsbooks face. +EV for US licensed sportsbooks, as competing substitutes face restrictions; −EV for Kalshi and Polymarket. An amendment from Brazil's PL party goes the other way, removing derivative contracts from the betting definition and placing them solely under the securities regulator CVM, so the proposed US approach points in the opposite direction. PH operators should treat prediction-market products as gambling products for compliance purposes. Read original →

SIGNAL 04 · UK · TAX-LED JOB CUTS

Flutter may cut 1,400 UK&I jobs after online casino tax rose to 40%, and the Oct 28 Budget could raise machine duty.

Flutter (owner of Paddy Power and Sky Bet) announced on 20 October a restructuring of its UK and Ireland business affecting Leeds, Dublin and offshore hubs in Porto, Portugal and Cluj-Napoca, Romania. About 15% of its roughly 9,000 UK&I staff could go, including more than 450 roles at risk in Leeds. Costs of US$80–90 million fall in Q4 and are already in the guidance updated at Q2. iGamingExpress adds that Sky Bet's Malta office, with about 50 jobs, will close and move to Gibraltar. Remote gaming duty (RGD, on online casino and slots) rose from 21% to 40% in April, and general betting duty on online sports bets goes from 15% to 25% from April 2027. evoke (owner of William Hill) reported Q3 revenue of £406 million on 23 October, down 7%, or 2% after adjusting for about 270 fewer shops, and its CEO publicly urged the government not to raise taxes again. The Times reports the Treasury is studying a doubling of machine games duty (MGD) bands, which the industry estimates could close almost 3,000 more betting shops.


Bottom line: UK tax rises are affecting financial results and staffing. The largest operator is absorbing the cost through job cuts and moves to cheaper hubs; industry commentators also cite AI and competition from challenger brands. If machine duty doubles on 28 October, betting shops are next to shrink. −EV for retail-heavy evoke and Betfred; relatively +EV for lean online-only challengers. For the Philippines, PAGCOR's income is already below target this year, and the UK sequence of "higher tax, job cuts, players drifting to the black market" is a concrete case PH operators can cite when discussing fees with the regulator. SIGNAL 05 · US · SWEEPSTAKES CASINOS

Oklahoma bans sweepstakes casinos from Nov 1 as Stake.us exits early; Florida presses Google and Meta over ads.

Sweepstakes casinos run a dual-currency model: gold coins can be bought, while sweeps coins are only "given away" or earned free and can be redeemed for cash, which lets them operate in states without legal real-money online casinos. Oklahoma's SB 1589 became law after lawmakers overrode the governor's May veto (34–10 in the Senate, 68–19 in the House) and takes effect on 1 November. It brings dual-currency systems into the gambling definition, with fines of US$500–2,000 and possible jail, and game developers and payment processors may also face criminal liability. Operators are already moving: ACE Casino left on 6 October, Baba Casino ended sweeps coins on 21 October, Coin Wizard closes on 28 October, Stake.us stops offering games after 30 October but still allows redemptions, and Modo stopped selling sweeps coins and kept gold-coin play only. Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier, who sued Stake.us, VGW and payment processors in August, wrote in late September to Google, Meta, Reddit, Snap and TikTok saying they may be illegally running ads for Chumba, LuckyLand, Global Poker and Stake to Floridians, and asked for written answers on their safeguards by 23 October. No platform replies or next steps from Florida had been reported at press time.


Bottom line: Sweepstakes enforcement now extends beyond operators to suppliers and advertising: Oklahoma brings payment processors and game suppliers within the scope of criminal liability, while Florida is holding ad platforms to account. Stake.us belongs to the same group as Stake, which operates in Brazil and the Philippine grey market, and other markets could readily adopt this approach to enforcement against platforms, payment firms and promoters. +EV for US licensed online casinos; −EV for sweepstakes operators, their game suppliers and affiliates. For the Philippines, once big ad platforms are told to block gambling ads by region, Meta and Google reviews of gambling creative will tighten across the board, so PH licensed brands should allow time for rejected creative and slower approvals; suppliers that also serve sweepstakes platforms should have their US exposure checked before deals. Regulation−NegativeCuraçao · Licensing portal

Curaçao confirms a nine-month licence portal breach and retires the system.

Gaming.net · Casinos.com · Sumsub · 10/19–10/21

The Curaçao Gaming Authority (CGA) found on 17 September that a German security researcher had opened an account under a false identity in December 2025 and could then access the externally managed licensing portal and its database for nine months. The CGA says its core infrastructure was not confirmed compromised but cannot yet establish what was taken. It has permanently retired the portal, launched a temporary system on 19 October and is preparing an in-house platform. Existing licences remain valid and compliance duties continue, but the digital seal that proves a domain is licensed "will be affected", and the CGA says it will soon offer another way to prove licensing. It has filed complaints in Curaçao, Germany and the Netherlands and hired firms to remove leaked data.


Bottom line: Many offshore platforms selling into Asia and Latin America hold Curaçao licences. What leaked is licensees' confidential data, and with the seal temporarily broken, players and payment providers cannot easily check which sites are really licensed. −EV for offshore platforms on Curaçao licences, as payment and ad-platform checks tighten. Relatively +EV for PH licensees: PAGCOR's licence-check app is coming, so "locally licensed and verifiable" carries more weight now, and creative can show the PAGCOR licence number.

Regulation○NeutralUK · Withdrawal delays

L&L Europe pays £900,000 over a one-withdrawal-at-a-time rule.

Casinos.com · Gaming.net · 10/21

The UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) announced on 21 October a £900,000 (about US$1.2M) settlement with L&L Europe in lieu of a fine. The failings ran from October 2024 to October 2025 across seven UK casino sites: the terms allowed players only one pending withdrawal at a time, which was ruled unfair under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, and some players had every fifth or tenth withdrawal manually reviewed, which the operator accepted delayed payouts. The UKGC also cited weak risk-based anti-money-laundering controls and missed signs of gambling harm. The operator says it has changed its procedures.


Bottom line: The UK regulator treats slow withdrawals as a breach in its own right. A parallel in the Philippines is that BingoPlus is running many Meta ads this week promising "100% compensation if your withdrawal is delayed, up to PHP5,000" (see §5), a sign that payout speed is what players care about most and what regulators watch most easily. +EV for operators with stable payout processes. Action: check terms for "one at a time" limits or manual reviews that cause delays; payout claims in creative must match what the operation can deliver.

Regulation○NeutralUS · New Jersey

New Jersey writes data-driven problem-gambling checks into its rules.

CDC Gaming · Bettingnews · 10/20–10/22

New Jersey Attorney General Jennifer Davenport and the Division of Gaming Enforcement (DGE) announced on 20 October that using data to identify problem gamblers is now written into state regulations, which officials call the first legally enforceable standard of its kind in the US. Online operators must monitor accounts against set behavioural criteria, review accounts that cross thresholds and offer support scaled to risk, using live chat or phone calls for serious cases and stopping play where needed; each online operator must appoint a responsible gaming lead. The rules cover online operators only, not land-based casinos. The state also announced free access to RecoverMe, a cognitive behavioural therapy app. The rules build on a DGE pilot from February 2023.


Bottom line: New York and Massachusetts are moving on AI-personalised promotions this month, while New Jersey requires operators to use data to protect players. Both approaches point to the role of data in identifying risk. Neutral for large operators that already run risk models, −EV for smaller ones that must add systems and staff. PH operators can build the same behavioural indicators now so they are ready if PAGCOR follows.

Regulation−NegativeNetherlands · Unlicensed fine

Dutch regulator fines EOD Code a record €37.25M over 12 unlicensed sites.

Gaming.net · Ksa · 10/22

The Dutch regulator Kansspelautoriteit (Ksa) announced on 22 October a €37.25 million (about US$43M) fine on Costa Rica-registered EOD Code SRL, its highest ever for unlicensed gambling. The 12 sites include instantcasino.com, skyhills.com and goldenpanda.com; Dutch players could register with a Dutch address, deposit through Dutch banks and crypto, saw the Netherlands preselected on sign-up and got answers in Dutch. Several Dutch influencers promoted Skyhills on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok and have received separate penalty orders. Using search volume, the Ksa estimated EOD Code's 2025 Dutch GGR at €108.4 million, implying a €292.6 million fine, but the legal cap of 10% of worldwide turnover reduced it to €37.25 million. Aggravating factors included no visible age checks, a €5 monthly fee on dormant accounts, a €4,000 daily withdrawal cap and keeping the balance of players who request permanent self-exclusion.


Bottom line: European regulators are now sizing fines by what an unlicensed operator earns from their country and punishing the influencers who promote it. These offshore groups, often on Curaçao or Costa Rica registrations, are the same ones working grey markets in Asia and Latin America. +EV for licensed operators as grey rivals' costs rise; −EV for offshore platforms that rely on influencer traffic. For the Philippines, if PAGCOR copies "fine by revenue and fine the influencer too", brands should check that a KOL has not promoted unlicensed platforms before signing.

Regulation−NegativeAustralia · Influencer ads

Australia warns two ex-AFL players over Vegastars videos; one was paid A$120,000.

Gaming.net · ACMA · 10/22

The Australian Communications and Media Authority (ACMA) issued formal warnings on 22 October to former AFL players turned influencers Mitch Robinson and Rhys Mathieson for posting videos on Instagram and a YouTube podcast in March and April 2026 promoting Vegastars, a prohibited online gambling service; the videos showed the site's name, logo, address and "wins". Bottom line: ACMA has extended enforcement from platforms to individual influencers, and "I didn't know it was illegal" is no defence. Neutral to +EV for licensed operators as grey platforms lose traffic channels; −EV for offshore platforms that depend on influencers. Palace press officer Claire Castro said on 20 October that President Marcos wants remaining Philippine offshore gaming operators (POGOs) and other illegal activities wiped out; Winston Casio of the Presidential Anti-Organized Crime Commission (PAOCC) had said some POGOs still operate despite the 2024 ban. Regulation−NegativeChina · Traffic hijacking

Hijacked routes sent Chinese users to gambling sites, CNCERT says.

Allin News (citing CNCERT) · 10/21

According to Allin, China's national computer emergency response centre (CNCERT) warned that users in several provinces have had web requests redirected to gambling sites. Attackers registered false address prefixes in the Internet Routing Registry (IRR), announced them with short paths from autonomous systems (AS) they controlled to hijack part of China's domestic traffic, then tampered with DNS answers to point domains at gambling sites or malicious DNS servers. Officials traced the tampering to AS 402425, registered in April 2026 by QOREXA LABS, a US company with bitcoin-related businesses. CNCERT told network operators to tighten route filtering and remove malicious routes, and urged users to use trusted DNS services.


Bottom line: Grey gambling platforms' acquisition tactics have gone from buying ads and using agents to hijacking network traffic, which will intensify China's crackdown on offshore gambling and related payments. −EV for offshore platforms targeting mainland players; indirectly −EV for Asian licensees, because Chinese crackdowns usually coincide with renewed POGO and cross-border payment scrutiny in the Philippines (see the POGO card in this section). Licensed brands should monitor their domains and DNS against copycats and hijacking.

Regulation−NegativeSouth Korea · Casino laundering

Korean police bust a KRW28.7B casino laundering ring using two-way baccarat bets.

Allin News (citing Chosun Ilbo) · 10/21

According to Allin, citing Chosun Ilbo, Busan police sent 54 suspects to prosecutors on 15 October, 14 of them detained. Between October and December 2025, the group took KRW28.7 billion (about US$20M) from an overseas investment scam with 555 victims, turned it into cashier's cheques and casino chips at Kangwon Land and several foreigner-only casinos, and had two members bet Banker and Player at the same baccarat table to cancel out losses before cashing out the chips. It recruited VIP players able to bet big and Koreans with foreign permanent residency who could enter foreigner-only casinos, and switched to cashier's cheques after some casino accounts were frozen. Police seized about KRW111 million in cash and luxury goods, froze about KRW390 million in property and urged casinos and the culture ministry to tighten monitoring of large bets and withdrawals.


Bottom line: Betting both sides in baccarat is a laundering method common to land-based and online casinos across Asia, and multiple accounts make it easier online. Cases like this prompt regulators to focus on betting-pattern monitoring. Neutral to −EV for Asian casinos and online operators as compliance costs rise. PH online operators should add alerts for "opposite bets in the same round" and "high turnover with little net result followed by an immediate withdrawal" to their risk rules, ready to show PAGCOR or the AML council if asked.

Linked wallets drove $108M of FIFA partner ADI Predictstreet's $167M volume.

A Wall Street Journal investigation found that about US$108 million of ADI Predictstreet's US$167 million trading volume through late September came from a cluster of wallets linked to the company, funded by a single wallet that also collected the platform's fees; since late August, 795 wallets accounted for 99% of volume. Sports○NeutralUK · Election-date bets

Three UK insiders who bet on the election date get suspended sentences.

Gaming.net · 10/23

Southwark Crown Court sentenced Anthony Lee, Laura Lee and Amy Hind on 23 October after they pleaded guilty to cheating under section 42 of the Gambling Act 2005 for betting on the 2024 general election date using confidential information. Anthony Lee, then the Conservative Party's director of campaigning, got 10 months and two weeks suspended for 12 months plus 100 hours of unpaid work; his wife Laura Lee, the party's head of international and a candidate that year, got four and a half months suspended; Hind got six months suspended.
