This week's competitive intelligence: TOP 3

11/21 verification scope

This issue re-pulled all 20 Meta advertiser accounts and re-read the Winpot home and promotions pages, Codere's promotions list and sports welcome offer, and Caliente's sports and casino promotions lists. Item-by-item status: verification log and sources.

1. Meta's library shows no live ads for 14 advertiser accounts. On 11/21, with the country set to Mexico and status to active ads, 14 of 20 accounts returned "no ads match your search"; 12 of them had ads on 11/14, with headers totalling about 1,261. All four group-B P-06 brands (Brazino777, Betmaster, BetMexico, Jugabet) are at zero; in the Caliente family only Caliente Sports is live, while Casino Caliente (~280), CalienteCasino.mx and Winner are at zero; Playdoit kept only its sports account. Strendus, Betway, Megwin and offshore BC.Game are also at zero. Caliente Sports, Winpot and Codere loaded normally in the same session, so a site-wide loading failure is ruled out. Evidence A (Ad Library; Betmaster and Casino Caliente also checked under all and inactive status). Assessment: in Mexico the library keeps only live non-political ads, so stopped ads vanish and the library cannot show whether an advertiser paused, an account was restricted or the platform removed ads. Five watchlist brands going to zero in one week is larger than Brazino777's single-brand swing in W41-42. Cause unknown; recheck next week. If the platform tightened, Betroia's own accounts sit under the same rules and their delivery status should be checked internally. Betmaster library · Casino Caliente library

2. Winpot launched 34 new "$500 no-deposit" ads in one week. 17 started on 11/17 and 11 on 11/19; header about 110 (about 100 last week). Each creative is tied to a slot title (5 Lions, Big Juan, River Gold, Fortune Garuda and others), with copy such as "$500 sin meter nada" and "Instala la aplicación ahora y obtén $500 de regalo, ¡sin depósito!"; 29 point to the App Store or Google Play. On 11/21 the home page showed "500 GRATIS + 100% BONO EN SU PRIMER DEPÓSITO" in casino and sports versions; the promotions page lists a 100% first-deposit match up to $10,000. Evidence A (Ad Library, home and promotions pages); wagering, cash-out cap and delivery of the $500 were not found on public pages, so the terms are unverified. Assessment: Winpot uses the same TaDa content and paid acquisition as Betroia, and an install-for-$500 hook brings the new-player entry bar down to no deposit. Neither audience overlap nor conversion has been verified; in the week group B went dark, Winpot is the like-for-like rival still adding volume. Winpot library · Winpot website

3. Supreme Court makes a Codere casino unit post a guarantee. Per El CEO, summarized by iGaming.mx on 11/20, the full Supreme Court on 11/18 unanimously denied an amparo to Operadora Cantabria, which runs Codere's land-based casinos in Mexico. The company is litigating a SAT claim of MXN 546m covering 2013 IEPS and related charges; the court held that halting collection requires a guarantee. Evidence B (press reports; no court press release, case number or ruling text located; no Codere statement). This is separate from the MXN 1.27bn tax case reported in May 2025. Assessment: litigation does not stop seizures without a guarantee, so the cash pressure falls on Codere's land-based business; Codere Online still opened 22 new Meta ads this week with no visible effect on online marketing. The same enforcement line applies to every permit holder under the 50% IEPS. iGaming.mx 11/20

  • Supreme Court guarantee case: see TOP 3 item 3. iGaming.mx 11/20
  • 2027 tax package: public coverage of the package passed by the Senate on 10/27 shows no gambling provision, so IEPS stays at 50%; any effect comes through income-tax limits on deductions and loss carry-forwards. DOF text not reviewed. iGaming.mx 11/17
