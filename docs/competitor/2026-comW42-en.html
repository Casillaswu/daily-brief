§02Strategic Read · 5Q

Strategic read · five questions (neutral)

Q1
Market space

September inflation was 7.2% year on year, the highest since March 2023, with core at 4.7%. Nomura expects a quarter-point hike from the BSP (Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, the central bank) on October 22; the policy rate is 5%. Maybank's 10/5 report already named squeezed disposable income as a reason for cutting DigiPlus revenue forecasts, with H1 revenue down about 31% to ₱32.9B. (Inquirer 10/8; the inflation figures are single-source)

Read: Both sources suggest pressure on players' disposable income. If that pressure continues, returning players may not drive Q4 market growth, and brands may face stronger competition for player budgets.

Q2
Player focus

This week BingoPlus's ads talk about three things at once: low turnover, fast withdrawals, funds protected, with high turnover and hard withdrawals as the foil.

Read: the leader has decided these are what players care about most when choosing a platform. Whether they match actual player complaints cannot be checked without social data this issue; no conclusion.

Q3
Unmet gaps

This issue's sources show no new progress on the six existing gaps: plain-language compliance creative, alternative payment how-tos, promo term calculators, KYC walk-throughs, licence verification explainers, and retention content beyond promos.

Two more specific gaps this week: turnover multiples and withdrawal times. BingoPlus says "low or no turnover" and "fast withdrawal", but the creative shows no multiple and no minutes.

Read: whoever first publishes turnover multiples and payout minutes as checkable figures owns the "transparent" slot.

The creative-level constraint still applies. ASC Memo 2026-008, effective June 24, bars showing or mentioning roulette, baccarat, blackjack, slot machines, cards, chips and dice, or similar casino games and paraphernalia, in ads on any medium.

Q4
Price structure

Still two models: DigiPlus runs events and VIP rewards, the rest run rebates. Two new wordings this week: BingoPlus uses "up to ₱X" on its VIP reward tier and lists a 3.5% game rebate; ArenaPlus keeps "up to ₱X" with no percentages.

Under current PAGCOR rules, cash rebates on wagers or deposits are capped at 1.5% (excluding table and arcade games), and cashback on net losses at 15%. Which games the 3.5% covers, and whether they are an exception, is not stated.

Read: percentages and peso caps side by side make players' comparison harder. Whether either is acceptable has no public interpretation yet (see §06 threat 2).

Q5
Differentiation

This week's routes: BingoPlus moves from awards sponsorship to VIP rewards and low turnover, ArenaPlus writes "casino too" into its ads, Bloomberry opens a live and creator platform. No new moves by CasinoPlus, PlayTime, GameZone, OKBet, Bet88 or OKADA PLAY were recorded in this issue's sources; coverage is limited.

Conclusion: the leaders emphasise reward and withdrawal terms, while the new entrant from a big operator emphasises social content. Q4 comes down to two things: how checkable terms such as turnover and withdrawals are, and creator availability.

Proposal: two things. First, fix the turnover multiple on welcome and reload offers before deciding to join the "low turnover" story. Second, ask about exclusivity before negotiating creator rates. These are suggestions, not conclusions.

§06Implications · Threats / Opportunities

Implications (threats / opportunities)

Entry parameters (all internal estimates)
  • Plan ARPU at ₱805, with room to fall: Maybank puts DigiPlus monthly spend per active user at about ₱805 against an assumed ₱1,250; after 7.2% inflation in September, ₱805 may still be high. Use ₱805 as base case, ₱1,250 as upside, and add ₱725 (another 10% lower) as a conservative case. Basis in Q1 and the §04 cards.
  • Budget acquisition cost to rise: S&P sees the leader's EBITDA margin falling from ~18% to 14.5-16% on higher acquisition and retention cost after delinking; Maybank cites 16.7% actual for H1.
  • Player scale reference: DigiPlus Q2 MAU (monthly active users) about 5.75M (BingoPlus ~3.35M, GameZone ~1.83M, ArenaPlus ~570K).
  • The rebate ceiling is regulatory: 1.5% cash rebate on wagers or deposits (excluding table and arcade games), 15% cashback on net losses; the scope of BingoPlus's 3.5% game rebate is not stated.
  • Creative constraints: ASC Memo 2026-008, effective 2026/06/24, bars showing or mentioning casino games and paraphernalia in ads.
  • Deadlines: B2B accreditation grace ended 9/30, 12 days ago, with no public decommission list; BSP comments closed 10/2, 10 days ago, no final circular seen, and the BSP policy meeting is 10/22; MGF phase 2 on 2027/01/01, 81 days out.
ThreatsThreats
  • High-turnover welcome offers will be compared. BingoPlus already shows "low or no turnover" in ads with high turnover as the foil. Cost of inaction (internal estimate): an entrant whose turnover multiple is clearly above what rivals claim could see 15-30% lower first-deposit conversion on the same placement.
  • VIP-tier percentages above 1.5% make copying risky. The 3.5% game rebate is above the current 1.5% cash-rebate cap, and its scope awaits PAGCOR's reading. Cost of inaction (internal estimate): if copied creative is asked to be revised or pulled, rework and resubmission take about 1-2 weeks of dead airtime.
  • Two DigiPlus brands talking up casino games crowd the casual audience. ArenaPlus opened 63 ads in five days, BingoPlus opened a VIP tier, and GameZone at ~1.83M MAU sits in the same casual audience. Cost of inaction (internal estimate): if an entrant's lead flight collides with theirs, click cost on casual-audience placements could rise about 10-20%.
  • Inflation pushes players' disposable income lower still. September inflation was 7.2% and the BSP may hike on 10/22. Cost of inaction (internal estimate): each further 10% fall in ARPU lengthens payback by about 11%, before any retention difference.
  • Creator and host slots get booked by a big licensee first. OnlyFun.PH wants more hosts, creators and streamers. Cost of inaction (internal estimate): if the lead flight needs top-tier creators, rates could be about 10-30% higher and slots may need locking a month earlier.
OpportunitiesOpportunities
  • Publish the turnover multiple as a number (internal estimate ~₱30K-₱80K / one-off). BingoPlus says "low or no turnover" without a multiple; showing the multiple and a worked example fills the gap. Note: examples must match the terms word for word, or clarity becomes a new source of complaints.
  • Make withdrawal time a checkable number (internal estimate ~₱40K-₱100K testing and creative / 1 month). The leader says "fast withdrawal" with no minutes. Note: payments and withdrawals must reliably hit the number first; once published, it has to be honoured.
  • Discuss creator partnerships early and assess longer terms (internal estimate ~₱50K-₱150K in fees / 3-6 months). OnlyFun.PH plans to expand creator partnerships; start discussions early. Note: check the creator has no exclusive deal with a licensed platform, and check live content against the ASC memo and PAGCOR ad rules.
  • Offer supplier-accreditation checks as a service (internal estimate ~₱20K-₱60K staff time / one-off). The B2B grace has passed and no decommission list has appeared; build an accreditation status list of lead games for partner platforms. Note: rely on the platform's written confirmation; do not judge status yourself.
