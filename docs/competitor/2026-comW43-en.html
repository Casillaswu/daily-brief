§02Strategic Read · 5Q

Strategic read · five questions (neutral statements)

Q1
Market space

In an interview, PAGCOR set a target of Q3 or Q4 2027 for its casino exit, with the GCG's decoupling recommendation due at the Palace within October. It has 35 casinos, expects ₱20-30 billion from the sale, and licensees have shown interest; Casino Filipino's 2026 loss is expected to be at least ₱7 billion. (Philstar 10/14, single full source)

Judgment: This restructures the casino business; online licences, rebate caps and ad rules did not change this week. Any effect on the online market is indirect and depends on Palace approval. The pressure from September's 7.2% inflation and the BSP 10/22 meeting (covered last issue) has no new data this issue.

Q2
Player focus

BingoPlus's focus this week is withdrawals: check withdrawal time, follow its status, claim compensation for a delay. ArenaPlus's sports clips cover a PVL rules explainer, a women-in-sport topic and a basketball tutorial.

Judgment: The leader treats the withdrawal experience as a player worry. This issue has no community data to establish whether players actually complain about it, so no conclusion is drawn.

Q3
Unmet gaps

Last issue identified two gaps: turnover multiples and withdrawal speed. BingoPlus has partly addressed the withdrawal-speed gap: there is delay compensation, but "Fast Withdrawal" still carries no minutes figure. On turnover multiples, no brand's new creative this issue states a multiple.

Judgment: Stating checkable payout times and turnover multiples first would help a brand stand out through clearer terms. A new gap is the official domain: with ArenaPlus's added domains, players find it harder to tell the official site.

The creative limit still applies. ASC Circular 2026-008, in force since June 24, bars ad material in any medium from showing or mentioning roulette, baccarat, blackjack, slot machines, cards, chips or dice and similar casino games and devices.

Q4
Offer structure

Still two types: DigiPlus leads with campaigns and VIP rewards, the rest with rebates. This week BingoPlus adds a format: dated short windows (10/15-10/20), a ₱100 login bonus and a 20% first-deposit bonus; ArenaPlus adds an "Up to P888 Cashback" button line to the welcome creative.

Under PAGCOR's current rules, e-games operators' cash rebates on turnover or deposits are capped at 1.5% (casino tables and arcade-type games excepted), and cash back on net losses at 15%. How "100% compensation of the withdrawal amount" is classed is not stated in the creative.

Judgment: Compensation, dated rewards and rebates coexist, so players find them harder to compare. There is no public explanation of whether this is compliant (see §06 threat 2).

Q5
Differentiation

This week's routes: BingoPlus moves from reward tiers to withdrawal compensation and dated windows, ArenaPlus adds volume, domains and content-style ads on its existing lines, PAGCOR outlines a target casino-exit timetable in an interview. This issue's data records no new moves for CasinoPlus, PlayTime, GameZone, OKBet, Bet88 or OKADA PLAY; coverage is limited.

Conclusion: The leader competes on how checkable withdrawal-type terms are, the other DigiPlus brand on creative volume. Q4 competition comes down to whether withdrawal and turnover numbers can be written down, and how recognisable the official domain is.

Proposal: If withdrawal speed is a selling point, measure actual payout times before deciding on a compensation scheme. Put the official domain list prominently in ads and on the site. These are suggestions, not conclusions.

Card 3 · PAGCOR10/13-10/14
Mid impact (indirect)

PAGCOR targets H2 2027 for its exit from casino operations.

PAGCOR (Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corp., also the online gaming regulator) chairman and CEO Alejandro Tengco, speaking in Boracay on 10/13-10/14, said the GCG (Governance Commission for GOCCs) is expected to send its decoupling recommendation to the Office of the President within October. Decoupling separates PAGCOR's commercial casino business from its regulatory role so it focuses on oversight and stops competing with the operators it regulates. The target is to complete the exit from casino operations in Q3 or Q4 2027; after approval, "then we execute".
PAGCOR has 35 casinos, 12 run outright and 23 under revenue-sharing with private partners. It expects ₱20 billion to ₱30 billion from the sale, with no valuation fixed; several licensees have shown interest, including large Entertainment City operators, and Tengco expects current revenue-sharing partners to bid for the casinos they already run. Among wholly owned casinos Cebu earns the most, followed by Tagaytay, Angeles, Davao and Malabon. Casino Filipino's 2026 loss is expected to reach at least ₱7 billion, from about ₱6 billion in 2025, which Tengco attributes mainly to personnel costs.
IAG's headline the same day gives a 2026 loss of about US$111 million; its body was not read this issue. Manila Bulletin and GGRAsia ran the same story, but this issue only reached a verification page and saw headlines.
Judgment: This restructures the casino business; it changes nothing directly for online licensees, and does not touch online licences, rebate caps or ad rules. Two indirect points: with PAGCOR regulating only, its dealings with online operators and its rule interpretations may face fewer doubts about its "referee and player" role; and licensees that bid for casinos would divert capital, with no evidence of any effect on online budgets, so no conclusion. The entire timetable depends on Palace approval, which has not happened.

§06Implications · Threats / Opportunities

Implications (threats / opportunities)

Entry parameters (all internal estimates)
  • ARPU based on ₱805, with room to fall: Maybank puts DigiPlus monthly spend per active user at about ₱805 against an earlier assumption of ₱1,250; after September's 7.2% inflation ₱805 may still be high. The entry model uses ₱805 as base, ₱1,250 as the optimistic case and ₱725 (another 10% lower) as the conservative case. No new ARPU data this issue.
  • Acquisition cost assumed to rise: S&P puts the leader's EBITDA margin falling from about 18% to 14.5%-16% as broken attribution raises acquisition and retention costs; Maybank cites a first-half actual of 16.7%.
  • Player scale: DigiPlus Q2 MAU (monthly active users) about 5.75 million (BingoPlus about 3.35 million, GameZone about 1.83 million, ArenaPlus about 0.57 million).
  • Rebate caps follow the regulation: cash rebates on turnover or deposits 1.5% (casino tables and arcade-type games excepted), cash back on net losses 15%; how BingoPlus's withdrawal-delay compensation is classed is not stated.
  • Creative-level limit: ASC Circular 2026-008, in force since 2026/06/24, bars ads from showing or mentioning casino games and devices.
  • Deadlines: the B2B accreditation grace ended 9/30 (19 days ago) with no decommission list public; BSP comments closed 10/2 (17 days ago) with no final circular; BSP monetary policy meeting 10/22; the GCG decoupling recommendation is due at the Palace within October; the second minimum guaranteed fee step is 2027/01/01, 74 days out.
ThreatsThreats
  • Withdrawal experience becomes the yardstick; no matching mechanism is a handicap. BingoPlus now advertises delay compensation of 100% of the withdrawal, up to ₱5,000 per transaction. Cost of inaction (internal estimate): an entrant with no payout promise sees trust-style creative convert roughly 10-20% lower.
  • Compensation wording is unclassed; copying it may carry compliance risk. Whether "100% compensation of the withdrawal" falls under current rebate caps is not stated in creative and PAGCOR has not explained it. Cost of inaction (internal estimate): if copied creative must be amended or withdrawn, revising and resubmitting it takes about 1-2 weeks, leaving the campaign slot unused.
  • Both DigiPlus brands ramping at once crowd casual-player placements. ArenaPlus runs about 80 ads a week and BingoPlus opens dated offers; GameZone's about 1.83 million MAU sits in the same casual segment. Cost of inaction (internal estimate): a main launch colliding with the same flight sees casual-segment click costs rise roughly 10-20%.
  • Several landing domains make the official site harder to recognise and let look-alikes in. At least four ArenaPlus landing domains have been observed; ownership of the new one is unverified. Cost of inaction (internal estimate): after entry, mirror or look-alike sites in brand-term search need extra monitoring and takedown work, about ₱30K-₱80K a month.
  • Inflation pushes disposable income down further. September inflation 7.2%, a BSP hike possible on 10/22. Cost of inaction (internal estimate): every further 10% fall in ARPU lengthens payback by about 11% (retention differences excluded).
OpportunitiesOpportunities
  • State withdrawal speed as a checkable number (internal estimate ~₱40K-₱100K for tests and creative / 1 month). The leader has delay compensation but no minutes figure; measure real payout times first, then put them in creative.
