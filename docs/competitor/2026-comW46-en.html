§02Strategic Read · 5Q

Strategic read · five questions (neutral statements)

Q1
Market size

Q3 industry GGR was ₱83.72B (-11.41%), e-gaming ₱35.28B (-22.86%), licensed casinos ₱45.38B (-0.40%), 54.20% of the industry. (SiGMA 11/06) PAGCOR's chairman called the narrower decline encouraging.
The PAGCOR licensed-platform ID app targets December; no new item was read this week.

Inference: Online is still shrinking but more slowly; casinos are about flat. This is industry GGR, a different basis from PAGCOR's own revenue.

Q2
What players care about

GameZone's creatives turn to a song, dancing and a meet-and-greet while keeping zero-fee and no-agent-deduction wording; DigiPlus says it keeps investing in own content and retention.

Inference: No player-side data was read this week; the only read on attention comes from the brands' own creatives: local culture and social play.

Q3
Unmet gaps

The GameZone and CasinoPlus creatives reviewed this issue did not state withdrawal times or wagering requirements. The ad-content limit still applies: ASC Circular 2026-008, in force since June 24, bars ad creatives in any medium from showing or mentioning casino games and equipment such as roulette, baccarat, blackjack, slots, cards, chips and dice.

Inference: Publishing verifiable payout minutes and rollover is still the place to improve term transparency.

Q4
Offer structure

Welcome figure read this week: GameZone ₱8,800 (11/3-11/5 batches; the ₱6,888 version was not seen). ArenaPlus's ad library read 0, PlayTime was not rechecked, and CasinoPlus's new batch has no amounts. Under current PAGCOR rules, cash rebates on bets or deposits for e-games operators are capped at 1.5% (casino tables and arcade-type games excluded) and cash back on net losses at 15%.

Inference: Less pricing information this week comes from thinner reading coverage and creative refreshes, not from offers disappearing; no terms pages were read.

Q5
Differentiation

This week's routes: GameZone local cultural content, DigiPlus cost control and in-house content, OKADA PLAY a new entrant accelerating acquisition, CasinoPlus generic game creatives. ArenaPlus, PlayTime, OKBet and Bet88 were incompletely read or not rechecked.

Conclusion: this week's differentiation sits in cultural content, promo discipline and a new entrant's growth.
Proposal: if entering, test content-led acquisition with a low-cost UGC mechanic before adding rewards. A suggestion, not a conclusion.

Card 1 · PAGCOR11/06
Mid impact (market baseline)

PAGCOR says Q3 industry GGR fell 11.4%, e-gaming 22.9%.

New this week (reported 11/06).PAGCOR reported Q3 2026 industry gross gaming revenue (GGR) of ₱83.72B, down 11.41% from ₱94.51B a year earlier and below Q2's ₱88.13B. Electronic gaming was ₱35.28B against ₱45.73B, down 22.86%; the ₱10.45B drop is most of the total decline. Licensed casinos made ₱45.38B (-0.40%), 54.20% of the industry versus 51.49% in Q2; PAGCOR-run casinos ₱3.03B (-4.81%). The year-on-year drop narrowed from Q2's -20.33%.
Basis.This is industry GGR. It is not the same basis as W45 card 1 (PAGCOR's own revenue, nine-month e-games -37.23%), so the two declines cannot be compared or netted. SiGMA says e-gaming now covers E-Bingo, E-Games and bingo grantees, while earlier quarters also counted poker, so categories may differ; year-on-year uses PAGCOR's prior-year figures. Chairman Tengco blamed inflation and geopolitical uncertainty for weaker discretionary spending and called the slower decline encouraging.
Versus last week.The W45 follow-up "PAGCOR Q3 standalone figures" now has an answer: the industry-wide GGR decline narrowed from -20.3% to -11.4% year on year. Inference: the year-on-year decline narrowed; this is not a recovery, and Q3 was also below Q2.
Evidence grade: B.Unverified: PAGCOR release page URL; effect of the e-gaming category change; whether operators decelerated in step.
⚠️ Secondary relays of one PAGCOR release: SiGMA and Focus Gaming News agree on the figures; the PAGCOR release page itself was not retrieved. No social sampling.
Sources: Philippine Q3 GGR falls 11.41% as e-games weaken · SiGMA 11/06 · Philippine gaming GGR falls 11.4% in Q3 2026 · Focus Gaming News 11/06
Operating meaning (judgement): The market is still shrinking but more slowly; an entry model can move the player-pool assumption from accelerating decline to slowing decline, but should not assume recovery.For Client X: Observe — The basis is industry GGR, not our target share; next week check the PAGCOR release and operator Q3 disclosures (DigiPlus is out, see card 2).

Entry parameters (all internal estimates)

The entry model uses ₱805 as base, ₱1,250 as the optimistic case and ₱725 (another 10% lower) as the conservative case. No new ARPU data this week; Q3 industry e-gaming GGR fell 22.9%, so a downward revision remains possible.
  • Acquisition cost and promo spend:S&P estimated the leader's EBITDA margin falling from about 18% to 14.5-16%. This week DigiPlus reports a 22% Q3 margin and credits tighter advertising and promotional spend; that is the company's basis and its link to S&P's estimate is unchecked, so the conservative case still assumes higher acquisition cost.
  • Player scale reference:DigiPlus Q3 MAU (monthly active users) 5.9M, +3% quarter on quarter; monthly active bettors and depositors 4.9M. Q2 per-brand MAU was about BingoPlus 3.35M, GameZone 1.83M, ArenaPlus 0.57M; Q3 per-brand figures were not read this week.
  • Rebate caps follow regulation:Cash rebates on bets or deposits 1.5% (casino tables and arcade-type games excluded); cash back on net losses 15%.
