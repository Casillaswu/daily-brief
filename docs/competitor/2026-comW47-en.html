PAGCOR / Commission on AuditPlayer voice & risk · geo-fencing
High impact (compliance risk)
Date: 11/05
Headline: CoA: licensed sites took bets from abroad.
Background, added late (reported 11/05, missed in W46). BusinessWorld: the Commission on Audit (CoA), in PAGCOR's 2025 audit report, found that domains registered under PAGCOR-accredited gaming system administrators (GSAs) and licensed casinos were accessible from, or accepted bets placed, outside the Philippines, breaching the GSA accreditation framework in force since 2025/10/02, which limits online gaming to domestic players. No operators were named.
Other findings in the piece. PAGCOR accounting issues: ₱3.14B of transactions booked under accounts payable instead of the proper liability accounts; a ₱310.37M discrepancy in a ₱1.38B inventory account; ₱305.76M of payables inactive for at least 10 years; ₱407.41M of receivables uncollected for over a decade.
Versus last week. W46 missed this piece (published inside its window; BusinessWorld's nation section was not checked). It is added as background and carded because it changes the baseline: geo-fencing enforcement is likely to become a regulatory focus.
Operating meaning (judgement): With offshore access flagged by auditors, PAGCOR may require tighter IP and geolocation blocking; that affects offshore marketing traffic and agent channels, and fits Bet88's block page for non-Philippine IPs.
Evidence grade: B (media relaying the audit report)
Evidence limits: Which domains and operators are involved; PAGCOR's response and remediation deadline; whether it becomes sanctions on operators.
Sources: CoA says PAGCOR-linked sites took bets from users outside Philippines · BusinessWorld 11/05
⚠️ Single-channel: one BusinessWorld piece; the CoA report and PAGCOR's response were not read.
Proposal (for Client X): Test: Check our landing pages and app for blocking and messaging on non-Philippine IPs; do not route offshore ad traffic to betting pages.
PAGCORPlayer voice & risk · data security
Mid impact (compliance risk)
Date: 11/11
Headline: PAGCOR warns over an alleged restricted-list leak.
New this week (PAGCOR advisory 11/11, 6 PM Philippine time; relayed by media 11/12). PAGCOR advisory on its site: after an online post allegedly containing data from PAGCOR's National Database of Restricted Persons (NDRP), PAGCOR said its Computer Emergency Response Team (PAGCOR-CERT) is verifying authenticity, source and extent, and whether it relates to an earlier incident; there is no verified evidence of a new intrusion and the assessment continues. PAGCOR advised the public to watch for phishing, identity theft and fraud and not to open or share related files; privacy concerns go to its Data Privacy Office and updates will come through official channels.
Background. The NDRP was breached in September 2025; reports then said over 560,000 names were involved, and PAGCOR said the list was not a list of problem gamblers.
Versus last week. Not covered in W46. This is a new regulatory-side event, not directly tied to operator promotions, but the NDRP underpins licensed operators' exclusion and self-exclusion checks.
Operating meaning (judgement): If the leak is genuine, phishing that impersonates PAGCOR or operators may rise and player trust in the "licensed" label could suffer; not confirmed.
Evidence grade: A (PAGCOR official advisory)
Evidence limits: Whether the posted data is genuine or 2025 material; any new requirement for operator KYC and exclusion checks.
Sources: PUBLIC ADVISORY On the Alleged Online Disclosure of NDRP Information · PAGCOR 11/11 · PAGCOR warns public after alleged restricted persons leak · iGamingExpert 11/12 · Hacked PAGCOR list of restricted persons contains over 560,000 names but not a list of gamblers, agency says · asgam 2025/09/03 (background)
PAGCOR's original advisory read on follow-up; the iGamingExpert relay matches; no player-side sampling.

Insight 1 · Market size：No new industry GGR data this issue; W46's Q3 industry GGR of ₱83.72B (-11.41%) remains the latest baseline. PhilWeb's Q3 revenue +152.9% shows casino-backed online platforms expanding.

No player-side data was obtained this week; in the reviewed creatives, sports events and local stars remain the main attention entry points.
Insight 3 · Unmet gaps：None of the ArenaPlus or CasinoPlus creatives read this issue state rollover multiples or withdrawal times. ASC Circular 2026-008, in force since June 24, bars ads from showing or mentioning casino games and equipment such as roulette, baccarat, blackjack, slots, cards, chips and dice.
Publishing verifiable terms and payout times sets us apart more than raising reward size.
Insight 4 · Offer structure：Read this issue: ArenaPlus welcome ₱8,100, 40% first deposit, daily up to ₱2,888, weekly up to ₱60,000; GameZone ₱8,800; BingoPlus and CasinoPlus main creatives carry no amounts. Under current PAGCOR rules, cash rebates on bets or deposits for e-games operators are capped at 1.5% (casino tables and arcade-type games excluded), and cash back on net losses at 15%.
Price competition is concentrated in ArenaPlus; the single 5% creative needs tracking to see if it is replaced.
Insight 5 · Differentiation：ArenaPlus runs layered rewards plus NBA; BingoPlus event partnership and winner stories; GameZone music content; CasinoPlus a responsible-gambling headline; casino-backed platforms a shared provider.
The visible differences this week are mainly reward layers, event partnerships and responsible-gambling wording.
Proposal: add a responsible-gambling line to our main creatives and check our geo-blocking first; both are low cost and in line with regulation.
